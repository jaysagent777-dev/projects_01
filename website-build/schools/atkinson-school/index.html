Welcome to Atkinson Senior Secondary School

In June 1899, the Sisters of St. Ann — Mother Angela, Sr. Hyacinth and Sr. St. Charles — started a primary school of 16 pupils up to Class I, with help from the Honourable Mr. Atkinson, the then Collector of Krishna District. For this purpose they established their convent in Bezwada (Vijayawada) at the behest of the Bishop of Hyderabad.

Within a year the strength grew to 56, and the first inspection was held in 1900. Today Atkinson is home to 4,331 students and 127 staff members.
