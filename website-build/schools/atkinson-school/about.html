Our history since 1899.

A story of faith, perseverance and education in the heart of Vijayawada.

History

Milestones

1899

The Sisters of St. Ann start a primary school of 16 pupils in Bezwada (Vijayawada) at the behest of the Bishop of Hyderabad.

1900

Within a year, 56 students — and the school's first inspection.

Early 1900s

Raised to Middle School and then Secondary School status within four years.

1982

Sr. Stella D'Souza takes over, acquires land and builds six major buildings.

Today: 4,331 students and 127 staff members.

Vision & mission

Why we teach.

Vision

Quality education with special preference for the poor and the marginalised — instilling human values so students become instruments of social change.

Mission

To form young people of character, distinguished by academic and professional excellence, sound human values, holistic growth, selfless service and leadership.
