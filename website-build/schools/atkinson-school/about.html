The History of Atkinson Senior Secondary School

In June 1899, the Sisters of St. Ann — Mother Angela, Sr. Hyacinth and Sr. St. Charles — started a primary school of 16 pupils up to Class I, with help from the Honourable Mr. Atkinson, the then Collector of Krishna District. For this purpose they established their convent in Bezwada (Vijayawada) at the behest of the Bishop of Hyderabad.

Due to their dedicated labour, within a year the student strength swelled to 56, and the first inspection took place in 1900. A boarding was opened for out-station students and new classrooms were built. Within four years, Atkinson was raised to Middle School and then Secondary School status. It was a co-education school, and students sat the Madras Board Anglo-Indian High School Examination.

After 50 years the staff had grown to 15 and the students to 500 — and to 800 after 75 years. In the last 45 years progress has been rapid: the student strength has soared to 4,331, with 127 staff members. Sr. Stella D'Souza took over the school in 1982, acquired land and constructed six massive buildings.

Vision Statement

"We the Sisters of St. Ann of Providence in India, challenged by the life and mission of Jesus Christ, who is the incarnation of the compassionate love of the Father, re-dedicate ourselves with a creative fidelity towards the education of children and youth, with special preference to the poor and the marginalised, to liberate and empower them by imparting quality education, instilling in them human values, and thus enabling them to become instruments of 'Social Change'."

Mission Statement

To form the little ones, youth and marginalised into women and men of character, who will distinguish themselves by their academic and professional excellence, sound human values of love, peace and brotherhood/sisterhood, and holistic growth in their intellectual, emotional, physical and aesthetic abilities — with a spirit of selfless service and leadership.

Our Founders

The Sisters of St. Ann of Providence carry forward the intention of their founders, Marquis Carlo Tancredi and Marchioness Giulia Di Barolo. Atkinson itself was begun in 1899 by Mother Angela, Sr. Hyacinth and Sr. St. Charles.
