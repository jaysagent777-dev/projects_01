Admissions Open 2026 – 2027

Pre-KG to UKG — co-education · Classes I to VIII — only for girls

Every child seeking admission must be introduced in person to the Principal by the parents. Registration forms should be filled in and submitted with the following documents — incomplete forms will be rejected. The age for admission to L.K.G. is 3 years (by June).

  • Photocopy of the Birth Certificate from the Municipality, and Baptismal Certificate for Catholics
  • Copy of Aadhaar card
  • A self-addressed stamped envelope for a reply

Candidates for higher classes will be tested and admitted to the class for which they are found fit, with a transfer certificate from the school last attended. Students from other states must have the transfer certificate counter-signed by their state's Education Department. Admission is entirely at the discretion of the Principal.
