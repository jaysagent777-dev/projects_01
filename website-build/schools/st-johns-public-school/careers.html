Careers

St. John's welcomes applications from committed and dedicated individuals who can contribute to the high standards of excellence desired at all levels. The school emphasises employee involvement and provides equal growth opportunities to all employees.

We are looking for talented individuals who aspire to a career in education with good remuneration and scope for career growth. Interested candidates may send their applications at any time of the year; candidates will be contacted depending on openings.

The broad areas of appointment are Teaching Faculty, Office and Admin staff, Sports and Physical Educators, Special Educators, and Catering and Housekeeping staff.

To apply, send your resume to any St. John's location you wish to join, or email info@stjohnspublicschool.com. Shortlisted candidates will be contacted by telephone for a personal interview on campus.

Benefits: we conduct regular training programmes for professional growth.
