Activities

Creative and performing arts, clubs, field trips and more.

Creative & innovative platform

Arts that enrich children and communities.

St. John's believes that creative and performing arts enrich people and communities, and are critical to understanding global cultures and the common themes that unite societies.

An aesthetically designed indoor AV room hosts shows and small gatherings, and an open-air amphitheatre hosts annual day celebrations and cultural events.

Field trips

'Classrooms without walls': field trips, hikes, nature walks and treks to learn about the environment, geography, history and archaeology — and the value of sharing, caring and teamwork.

Art & music

Lessons by trained faculty in the Art and Music room, with Indian and western classical music. Exceptional work is displayed in the school museum.
