Activities

Creative and Innovative Platform

St. John's believes that creative and performing arts enrich people and communities, and are critical to an understanding of global cultures and the common themes that unite societies.

The school has established clubs and societies — music, theatre, dance, visual arts, pottery, debates, stamp collection, gardening, cooking, cultural and literary activities — that students and teachers design together. An aesthetically designed indoor AV room hosts shows and small gatherings.

Field Trips

Educational tours are 'classrooms without walls'. Students and teachers venture out for field trips, hikes, nature walks and treks to learn about environmental, geographical, historical and archaeological subjects — and the value of sharing, caring and teamwork.

Art Develops Ideas

The Art and Music room offers lessons by trained faculty, with training in Indian and western classical music. Exceptional work is displayed in the school museum, and an open-air amphitheatre hosts annual day celebrations and cultural events.

St. John's Educational Group — quality education in Vijayawada since 1985.
