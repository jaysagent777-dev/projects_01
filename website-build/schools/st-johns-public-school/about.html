Our story since 1985.

From a small school of 192 students to an educational group across Vijayawada.

History

Three decades of setting standards.

1985

St. John's founded at M.G. Road by Shri Thyagaraju Sahayam and Dr. T. Sudarsanamma, with 192 students.

Growth

New campuses at Gunadala and Satyanarayanapuram bring quality teaching to more of the city.

Today

An educational group offering SSC and CBSE from Nursery to Grade X.

Next

A new campus in Bhavanipuram.
