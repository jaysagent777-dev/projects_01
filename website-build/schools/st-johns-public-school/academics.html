Our approach

Traditional values, progressive learning.

St. John's firmly believes that academic excellence along with overall development of the child is the key to succeed in this competitive world. Taking best practices from both contemporary and traditional tenets of education, the school offers a custom-created programme following both SSC and CBSE syllabus from Nursery to Grade X.

Students become independent, keep their feet firmly on the ground, and learn to adapt and excel in their chosen fields anywhere in the world.

Early childhood programme

Radford, our play school under the aegis of St. John's, gives little ones a caring, secure start — colourful classrooms, activity rooms and the latest educational play systems.

Primary curriculum

Driven by children's natural curiosity: they explore, investigate, question and show what they learn in many ways, including dance, theatre and movement.

Parents as partners

Orientation programmes and regular parent–teacher meetings keep parents involved in their child's development.

Technology in learning

ICT labs, language labs and a digital library wing support learning, and parents can follow progress online.
