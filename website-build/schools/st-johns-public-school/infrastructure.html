Infrastructure

The premises of St. John's at Gunadala are vast and ideally located for easy access. The design and architecture are modern and aesthetic, and the campus facilities are apt for next-generation schooling.

Each classroom has been designed to provide a vibrant and safe learning environment — tested for adequate lighting and seating distance from the boards, with a colour scheme conducive to learning.

Child-Friendly Building and Furniture

The building follows the principle of "building as a learning aid", with plenty of open spaces for project work and open learning. Furniture is built from child-friendly materials.

Library

Books on literature, religion and technology in English, Hindi and more.

Computer Lab

Latest computers with inverter and UPS backup. Computer education from Class 1 to 10.

Multi-Purpose Hall

A large hall with stage, lighting and sound for cultural and co-curricular activities.

Green Park

Greenery all around for a healthy environment and fresh air.

Hygienic Water

RO-filtered, pure drinking water.

Grounds

For athletics and sports.
