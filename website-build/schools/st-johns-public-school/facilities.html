Facilities

Our facilities keep improving with every passing year. The success of St. John's prompted the management to spread quality teaching to M.G. Road, Gunadala, Satyanarayanapuram and Bhavanipuram.

Adopting the Latest Technology

Language labs support students from different regions, and parents can log in to follow their ward's progress online. Full-fledged ICT labs with the latest hardware and software offer a computer course that surpasses the board stipulation, with a digital library wing attached.

State-of-the-art Labs for Science

Science labs create the right ambience for experiments, with group projects, symposia and science fairs. The Library-Media Centre stocks books, reference materials, dailies and journals, and runs special library weeks.

Love for Reading

We confidently predict that technology will never replace the book. The Library-Media Centre features books, periodicals and quiet story-telling corners.

St. John's Educational Group — quality education in Vijayawada since 1985.
