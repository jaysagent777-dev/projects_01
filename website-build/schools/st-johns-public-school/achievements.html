Achievements

Our students in science, sport and the arts.

AcademicsAchievements
Science

103rd Indian Science Congress

P. Partha Koundinya of Class X won at district and state level in the National Children's Science Congress 2015, took first place at the National Council for Science & Technology Communication, and third place at the 103rd Indian Science Congress, Mysore (January 2016), on "Understanding Weather and Climate".

Sport

Wizards of St. John's

Winners of the City Level Inter-School 20-20 Cricket Tournament hosted by Nalanda Vidya Niketan — beating V.P. Siddhartha Public School and Delhi Public School on the way, and the hosts by 26 runs in the final. T. Raghu was Man of the Match and Man of the Tournament.

Arts

Elocution, essay & art

Students from Classes IV–X won prizes in elocution and essay writing at Sarvothama Grandhalayam, the Department of Posts' Design-a-Stamp contest, and an inter-school drawing competition.
